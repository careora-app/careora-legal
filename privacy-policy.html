Privacy Policy

Effective 17 November 2026

Careora is a personal care trial notebook for the iPhone you are using. This policy explains what Careora stores on that device, when it asks for hardware access, and how local sign-in works. Careora does not operate a Careora account server and does not upload your specimen notes, batch logs, ratings, or photos to a Careora backend.

Local sign-in

One-Tap Login opens a guest session stored only on this device. Careora keeps a local user identifier and a session flag in app preferences. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. That identifier is not sent to a Careora account server.

Log Out clears the session flag. Your archive of products, batches, experience logs, and photos stays on the device. Delete Account removes the local identifier and session, and permanently deletes the archive stored for this install.

Data on this device

Product and batch records, experience notes, ratings, timestamps, optional specimen photos, export caches, and preferences such as the task-switcher privacy scrim are stored in the app sandbox and local preferences. Removing the app, using Erase Local Archive, or deleting the account removes this local data from the install.

Demo specimen content shipped with the app is illustrative sample data. It is not networked social content and is not uploaded.

Camera

Camera access is requested only after you choose to capture a specimen photo for a record. Photos you accept are copied into the app sandbox. Careora does not scan the camera in the background.

Photo library

Photo library access is requested only after you tap Select from Photos on a trial log. Careora then presents the system photo picker so you can choose images to copy into the app sandbox. Careora does not scan your library in the background.

Microphone and speech

Microphone and speech recognition access are requested only after you tap Speak note on a trial log to dictate a short sensory reaction into text. You can type the note instead. Careora does not record audio in the background.

Export

JSON, CSV, and ZIP exports you create stay under your control. Sharing an export uses the system share sheet to destinations you choose. Careora does not transmit exports to a Careora server.

Purchases

Credit vials are consumable in-app purchases processed by Apple. Careora keeps the resulting Credit balance on this device. Credits are used for Save Active (10 Credits after confirmation) and for including specimen photos in an export package (5 Credits after confirmation). Drafts stay free. The balance is not restored after you delete the app, erase its data, or delete the local account. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

Notifications

After you enter the main archive, Careora may show the system notification permission prompt. If you allow notifications, the device token is stored on this device so Careora can register for remote notifications.

What we do not collect

Careora does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not read the advertising identifier. Careora does not include advertising SDKs. There is no Careora analytics server receiving your notes. A push token stays on this install unless you delete the account or erase local data.
