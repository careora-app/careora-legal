What Careora is

Careora is a personal notebook for recording personal care product trials, batch notes, ratings, and optional specimen photos on your device. It is not a medical device, not clinical advice, and not a networked social network.

Your records

Everything you enter stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself. You are responsible for the accuracy of what you record and for keeping your own backups. Demo specimens are sample content for illustration only.

Acceptable use

Do not use Careora to store unlawful content or to record information about other people without their consent. Keep exported files you share in line with the privacy of the people and products they describe.

Account

Sign-in is a local One-Tap Login guest session on your device. There is no password and no Sign in with Apple. You may log out at any time. Delete Account in Settings permanently deletes the local guest identity and the archive stored for this install.

Permissions

Camera access is optional and used only after you choose to capture a specimen photo. Photo library access is requested only after you tap Select from Photos, and Careora then presents the system picker for items you select. Microphone and speech recognition access are requested only after you tap Speak note to dictate text; you can type instead. After you enter the main archive, Careora may show the system notification permission prompt. Careora does not request App Tracking Transparency or read the advertising identifier.
